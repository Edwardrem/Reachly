Reachly

Every machine within reach.
Reach any machine from one place: a free, open-source Mac app with a terminal, file transfer and an AI assistant (Claude, Caimex or your own endpoint) that works on your machines under your rules.
No account. No server. No subscription.

Latest release · macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon and Intel · all releases. Unsigned for now: on first launch allow it with Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security (details).

Everything on your Mac

Hosts in a local database, secrets in the Keychain behind Touch ID. Nothing leaves the machine except the SSH connections you make.

Terminal you'd expect

Tabs, nested splits, true colour, 100 000 lines of scrollback, auto-reconnect after sleep, restored on relaunch.

Move between Macs

One encrypted .reachly file carries hosts, keys, snippets and themes. Import from ~/.ssh/config, Termius, PuTTY and Tabby.

Claude on your servers

Bring your own Anthropic or Caimex API key, or connect Claude Desktop and Claude Code over MCP. Every command passes your per-host guardrails and lands in an audit log.

See it in action

Every screenshot runs against a demo fleet. The hosts, keys and conversations are made up; your real hosts never leave your Mac.

What's in Reachly 1.0

  • An AI assistant that works on your machines: Claude, Caimex or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint lists your hosts, runs commands over SSH, reads and writes files, moves files between machines and writes PDF, Word, HTML, Markdown or CSV reports. Pick the provider and model in Settings → AI Integration.
  • You stay in control: each host is read-only, ask-every-time or allowlist, destructive commands always ask first, and an audit log records every command with its host, time, decision and exit status.
  • Attach images, PDFs, Word documents, logs or a whole folder to a question, or dictate it: speech is transcribed on your Mac.
  • A full terminal: tabs and nested splits, true colour, 100 000 lines of scrollback, auto-reconnect, snippets with placeholders, searchable history with exit codes and inline suggestions.
  • Every way in: jump hosts, HTTP and SOCKS5 proxies, port forwarding, a dual-pane SFTP browser, serial consoles for routers and boards, and imports from ~/.ssh/config, Termius, PuTTY, Tabby, AWS, DigitalOcean and Azure.
  • Keys and passwords in the Keychain behind Touch ID, hosts in a local database, and an encrypted .reachly file to move everything to another Mac. An MCP server lets Claude Desktop and Claude Code use the same tools and guardrails.

Updates

The app checks appcast.xml on this site once a day. Builds signed with a Developer ID install updates in place through Sparkle; unsigned builds offer the download instead. Enable the beta channel in Settings → Updates to follow pre-releases.
